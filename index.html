How we build

Capital, governance, development.

The Group invests in established platforms, supports experienced operating teams and expands through acquisitions, asset upgrades and new management models.

  1. 01

    Acquisition and financing

    Capital is directed to companies and projects with a clear market role, from Croatian hospitality and healthcare to new regional investments.

  2. 02

    Operating governance

    Portfolio companies retain specialist management teams, while strategy, financing and oversight are coordinated at Group level.

  3. 03

    Development and integration

    Each acquisition builds on capabilities already in place, extending destination expertise, distribution reach and experienced operating teams.

Value creation

Value is built in operations.

The clearest outcome of an investment is not a holding structure, but a better asset, a broader network and an operating platform ready for the next project.

Investing in the portfolio

Aminess Avalona opened in 2024, while Atea was refurbished and upgraded to a five-star campsite before reopening in June 2025.

Operating expertise

Aminess Hospitality Group manages owned, leased and partner properties, while Medika supplies the Croatian market through four distribution centres.

Governance and reporting

The consolidated financial statements are prepared under EU IFRS and published together with the independent report of Ernst & Young Audit.

Growth

From two platforms to a regional group.

Hospitality and healthcare remain the core, while transactions in 2025 and 2026 expanded the Group into Polish pharmacy retail and port logistics infrastructure.

Selected milestones, 2019–2026
2019Auctor Holding is established and becomes the Group's parent company.
2021Aminess becomes the majority owner of HTP Korčula and HTP Orebić.
2024The five-star Aminess Avalona Camping Resort opens on the island of Pag.
2025The Group's interest in Medika rises to 68.09%; Auctor Beta acquires a 25% interest in the owner of Poland's Gemini pharmacy chain.
2025Secured bonds of €40 million and CZK 1 billion are issued, both maturing in 2030.
2026Aminess expands to 33 properties across 12 destinations; HTP Korčula and HTP Orebić merge into Aminess.
2026Joint venture DD Rail Properties acquires 49.99% of Poland's OT Logistics.

Governance

The people accountable.

Auctor Holding is jointly controlled by Auctor Prime and JTPEG Croatia Investments. At year-end 2025, each held 45%, with Perrarus Holding holding the remaining 10%. The Board of Directors consists of Oleg Uskoković and Josef Pilka.

Questions and answers

The facts people ask about.

Concise, self-contained answers based on public company and regulatory sources.

What is Auctor Group?

Auctor Group is an investment group whose parent company, Auctor Holding, a.s., was established in 2019. Its two core operating platforms are hospitality through Aminess and pharmaceutical distribution and retail through Medika.

What were Auctor Group's 2025 results?

The Group generated €1.086 billion in consolidated revenue, €66 million in EBITDA and €20.6 million in net profit. Total assets at year-end were €1.043 billion.

Who owns Auctor Group?

As of 31 December 2025, Auctor Prime d.o.o. and JTPEG Croatia Investments, a.s. each held 45%, while Perrarus Holding a.s. held 10%. The Group is jointly controlled by Auctor Prime and JTPEG Croatia Investments.

Which sectors does Auctor Group operate in?

The core platforms are hospitality through Aminess Group and pharmaceutical distribution and retail through Medika Group. The Group also has a minority interest in Poland's Gemini pharmacy chain and a joint investment connected with OT Logistics.

What are the registered details of AUCTOR d.o.o. in Croatia?

AUCTOR d.o.o. for business consulting, Croatian tax number 19203050598, is an active company registered at Ulica kneza Branimira 71/E in Zagreb. Its sole shareholder is Auctor Holding, a.s.; its directors are Oleg Uskoković and Josef Pilka.
