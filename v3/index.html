Growth since 2019

From a single foundation to regional scale.

Hospitality and healthcare remain the core, with recent investments extending the Group into Polish pharmacy retail and port logistics.

  1. 2019

    Auctor Holding is established as the Group's parent company.

  2. 2021

    Aminess becomes the majority owner of HTP Korčula and HTP Orebić.

  3. 2024

    The five-star Aminess Avalona Camping Resort opens on Pag.

  4. 2025

    Auctor Beta acquires a 25% interest in the owner of Poland's Gemini pharmacy chain.

  5. 2026

    DD Rail Properties acquires 49.99% of Poland's OT Logistics.

Questions and answers

The facts people ask about.

What is Auctor Group?

An investment group with two core platforms: hospitality through Aminess and pharmaceutical distribution and retail through Medika.

What were the Group's 2025 results?

€1.086 billion in consolidated revenue, €66 million in EBITDA and €20.6 million in net profit.

Where does Auctor operate?

Across Croatia, the Czech Republic, Slovakia and Poland, with its principal operating platforms rooted in Croatia.
