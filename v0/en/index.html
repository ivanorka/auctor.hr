How we invest

The symbol also
describes how we work.

The circle is the origin, the line connects vision and execution, and A stands for authorship. We enter every investment in the same order — which is why we do not acquire companies simply to resell them.

  1. Circle — origin

    Acquisition

    We choose companies that already lead their markets but need the capital or structure for the next step. Our first investment was Aminess in 2019.

  2. Line — connection

    Investment

    We establish governance and invest in assets — new hotels, renovated campsites and logistics. Since 2019, approximately €170 million has been invested.

  3. A — authorship

    Ownership

    We remain the owner and put our name behind the business. Loan-to-value fell from 68.6% in 2019 to 47.4% in 2024, while EBITDA grew by 20% annually.

Growth

Six years
on the same course.

Each acquisition builds on the previous one by strengthening an existing destination, network or operating team.

  1. 2019.

    Auctor is established and Aminess acquired — the beginning of investment in hospitality.

  2. 2021.

    Acquisition of HTP Korčula and HTP Orebić.

  3. 2022.

    Hoteli Njivice and Romana; Aminess Khalani opens as the first five-star hotel.

  4. 2024.

    Nova Camping and AHM; Avalona Camping Resort opens on Pag.

  5. 2025.

    Meeting Point Croatia acquired, adding destination management capabilities.
